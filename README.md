# AI Partner — AI Boyfriend & Girlfriend App

A free AI companion app: chat, voice notes, 500 love GIFs, themes that match your partner, in 15 languages.

**Try it in your browser (no install):** https://rossycoder.github.io/ai-boyfriend-app/
**Download the Android APK:** https://github.com/rossycoder/ai-boyfriend-app/releases/tag/apk-v1.4

Features:
- 4 boyfriends and 4 girlfriends, each with their own look, voice and theme
- Instagram-style chat with reactions, emoji understanding, GIFs and voice
- He/she remembers you, wishes you good morning and happy birthday, and looks after you
- 100% free, no account needed, your data stays on your phone
