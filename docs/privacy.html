Privacy Policy — AI Boyfriend

Last updated: 11 November 2026

AI Boyfriend ("the app") is a romantic AI companion for adults (18+).

What we collect

  • Nothing is collected on our servers. The app has no account system and no analytics.
  • Your name, chosen boyfriend, language, chat history, bond points and "memories" are stored only on your device (app storage). Deleting the app deletes them.
  • When you send a chat message, the message text is sent to our AI text provider (Pollinations) only to generate his reply. We do not store, sell, or share your messages.

Permissions

  • Internet — needed to generate AI replies and nothing else.
  • The app does not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera.

Children

The app is for adults only (18+). It is not directed at children.
